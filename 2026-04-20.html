Restaurant Owner Opened on Easter to Feed Dying Man's Final Wish
Before Frank Ozimek passed away from cancer, he had one last request: he wanted the hospice nurses who cared for him in his final six weeks to be fed on Easter. His brother Ken tried to make it happen, but every restaurant in Lockport was closed for the holiday. He called one more — Tommy Milani at Sub Delicious. Without hesitation, Tommy set aside his own Easter plans, opened the restaurant, and prepared enough food to feed the entire nursing staff. "He was kind enough to push back Easter to him, put everything aside, open his restaurant, and make us food for the whole building down there," Ken said. "To me, it means the world to see that kindness, that greatness spread."
That's Frank living on through Tommy. 💛
