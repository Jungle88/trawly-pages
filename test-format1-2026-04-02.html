AI Tools
Most founders are using "AI agents" and "agentic AI" wrong — here's the actual difference
Akhilesh Kumar broke down the distinction that's tripping up everyone building with AI right now. An AI agent is a discrete task executor — you send it a job, it completes it. Agentic AI is a system that reasons, plans and adapts across a sequence of tasks without being re-prompted at each step. The difference matters enormously if you're trying to replace marketing or sales workflows rather than just automate single tasks.
